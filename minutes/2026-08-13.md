  - Intro revisions:

    - Susannah explained the remit for the Scope and work product - where this came from (prompted by feedback), and what was done (meetings on Zoom - group inputting into a revised introduction, and making the section introductions more succinct - meetings happened over the last two weeks):

    - Also included a better understanding of “our lane”

    - We also want to include ‘talking points’ to help us describe what the WSG do - to explain why we are different.

    - Susannah did an out loud readthrough to the group while screensharing for group opinions, ahead of sharing the document more widely

    - Covered the aims of the rewrite in going back to first principles and original references. Confirmed rewrite only covers 1.1 through 1.1.x

    - Susannah explained reasoning behind tweaking the previous definition of web sustainability - taking enough from those definitions to explain how the WSG are different but not seeking to comprehensively define web sustainability in all its forms. Original definition came from Sustainable Web Manifesto. Consistent feedback from readers that they don’t understand that definition and how our guidelines link to the web. Despite the time spent at this point, terminology used required readers to go down too many levels to understand.

    - Feedback following the read-through from Jen was that the revised introduction could help to better make the business case for web sustainability - it felt like something they could take to their leadership and they would ‘get it’

    - Alex - TAG and horizontal review have spoken previously about the interdependence with other work, is this called out here? Open to strengthening wording if necessary

    - Suggestion to add the word ‘equity’ after accessibility to emphasise we are covering other areas of impact and to convey the idea of ‘everything long-term’

    - Point raised to be consistent in use of WSGs (plural) and WSG (singular) - and the verbs that go with WSG. Suggestion to follow the WCAG convention- by referring to ‘WSG documents’ or repeating ‘guidelines’

    - Susannah reiterated our goal to get the intro to a state where it can be taken to people new to the topic to test it with - by Friday 14 August.
