  - **Conformance section** - should we have thresholds? Mandatory guidelines?

    - Tzviya: Section 1.4 right now goes through some basic conformance structure. Discussing with Alex yesterday, we originally thought to make something based of old Bcorp model. It was never documented. Sentence that people have most issues with is called “full conformance” … “A digital product or service fully conforms to WSG when it meets all success criteria across all guidelines”. … we know it can’t be “fully conformance” so we need to figure out how do we approach it

    - Alex: Idea would be to have some "mandatory guidelines” that we consider everyone should conform to. Minimum success criteria. As far as you get that + any else, in percentage, we can consider that conformance

    - Tim: Conformance vs continuous improvement? sustainability is journey and a lot of thing fall through the crack over time.

    - Tzviya: We generally say you conform with specification, you comply with regulation. She thinks they are not different. Part of the problem with WCAG was that you achieve A and keep improving but that is not recognized until you reach everything required for AA. We should implement a model of continuous improvement here.

    - Alex: we know that not everyone can take all the steps needed, even with percentage threshold. A lot of websites don’t even meet base WCAG. What we can do that we can specify when you can claim conformance, but you can also do partial-conformance. As far as you are meeting the baseline and we see progress, we are happy.

    - Mike: A lot of time in WCAG AAA is aspirational goal people never achieve. If they are doing a repeatable process to measure and provide measurements from one year to next, that is already great. More aligned with [EN 301 549](https://accessible.canada.ca/standards-and-technical-guides/standards-and-technical-guides-database/can-asc-en-301-5492024-accessibility-requirements-ict-products-and-services-en-301-5492021-idt/14-conformance)

    - Tzviya: if you look at WCAG, explanation for conformance is pretty useless. It does not explain conformance but what success criteria is. [EN 301 549 ](https://accessible.canada.ca/standards-and-technical-guides/standards-and-technical-guides-database/can-asc-en-301-5492024-accessibility-requirements-ict-products-and-services-en-301-5492021-idt/14-conformance) is legislation. We don't need to look at a11y as they didnt succeed there yet. **Is there a feeling that threshold is appropriate? (**if reading this and disagree, please reach out**)**

    - Susannah: how do we go about deciding on which guidelines are mandatory

    - Alex: Which percentages are thresholds?

    - Alex: We can leverage impact rating. Ones that have highest score can be mandatory

    - Sid: We can use some prio matrix: importance vs urgency. Low effort/high impact so people can get results fast

    - Tim: that is very good way to help people visualize

    - Emma: Long term/short time division

    - Alex: As part of IR we included a time frame. You can use a filter to see it fast and easy and with that we can quickly see what could potentially be mandatory

    - Susannah: Threshold criteria should be based on what are the most important goals of WSG. It is opportunity to us to emphasis what is most important

    - Tzviya: Practicalicities: where do we cut it off, at which number? Hosting section, short with big impact -> scores are all over the place. How does this apply to real world. Use sustainable hosting is a 9, which makes the cut, Use automation wisely is only a 4?

    - Mike: It comes down to how many we want to have. Should be good to have one for each section. Have something that people can commit to doing. Doable number for people to actually do it. Lets make it easy to start and accelerate beyond that

    - Susannah: Threshold, are there just guidelines or also emissions?

    - Tim: whatever we can do to support the idea of continuous improvement.

    - Sid: 80/20 rule. 80% of people will be applying 20% of guidelines. And 20% of people would apply 80% guidelines

    - Alex: This was done before the impact document. We basically polled the group and asked about their thoughts on what would be the main ones. Maybe we can nominate a few and go that direction.

    - Ines: Prioritizing based on CO2 is challenging, because we don’t have proper tools (yet). I like percentages. Why? I worked for big companies for a long time - they like to have badges and focus on improvement. They won’t say 49% WSG, but they will say Bronze (or similar). For basic guidelines - maybe we should have a mix of easy wins and the complicated ones? Green hosting is going to be a years-long process, but there need to be some easy wins with steady progress.

    - Susannah: Tiers have to include documented continuous improvement

    - Tzviya: how to balance documented tiers and stay out of measurement. We are talking percentages, not mandatory guidelines

    - Susannah: we can have bucket A/bucket B

    - Tzviya: you can have full compliance in Web Dev, not having touched any other sections.

    - Tim: Mike, did WCAG plan to introduce gold/silver/bronze.

    - Mike: it is probably easier to understand, but not a huge difference. There is discussion around it

    - Alex: with tiers people usually aim for the lowest they can go for. When it comes to WSG, we need something that is flexible enough and focuses more towards continuous improvement. With tiers, they will focus on what is the least amount of work and skip away.

    - Tzviya: incorporating continuous improvement into the requirements. Hidde has been involved in the conformance model so it would be great to involve him. Do we agree on not having mandatory or just go with percentages? Maybe we can re-write the draft on what conformance means.

    - Tim: there is CI included in the new Bcorp model

    - Ines: I have never seen people not trying to cheat the system any way they can. We should not put too much effort in trying to get a system people won’t cheat as that is inevitable.
