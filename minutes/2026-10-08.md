  - [**Conformance**](https://github.com/w3c/sustainableweb-wsg/pull/480) - Reminder to contribute

    - Alex confirmed old model was tailored on success criteria, guidelines, sections, then layering up conformity. Can’t expect people to conform to the full spec as this will be unfeasible. New conformance sets out continuous improvement baseline, conform to what you are able and provide evidence of progress. Show forward motion, achieve partial conformity.

    - Threshold percentage of 80% for conformance, a more realistic aim. Exclusions for guidelines that don’t apply to a particular organisation, so organisations aren’t punished for not conforming to guidelines they have no control over or aren’t involved in. Reduces the threat of potential gamification.

    - Tzviya called review of for preview/diff 1.4.2, 1.4.3, 1.4.4

    - Review wording and whether 80% threshold is fair and reasonable

    - Rose and Tim - consider failure and overscoring to ensure measurements are being applied properly

    - Susannah - is it possible to add another guideline around displaying sustainability statement. E.g. a point awarded for saying “We conform with WSG”

      - Alex confirmed an success criterion exists

    - Rose - concerns around orgs claiming conformance incorrectly. Issue is W3C isn’t a certification body, fears that awarding a point for this is open to nefarious deeds

    - Alex - don’t want to take on specific step around how to report. Existing things such as GRI, CSRD. Idea of WSG and reporting model is we ask, you provide evidence, that forms part of your reporting (e.g. CSRD). Falling in line with existing or forthcoming legislation. Don’t want to rewrite what already exists. We’re not about telling you how to report or comply, we’re just about saying you should provide the evidence how you see fit. We want transparency and evidence.

    - Rose - can we ask connections e.g. EMPCO? We assist regulators, how can we support them?

    - Tzviya - we are not badging, we provide the information that badging service may choose to use. **Separate conformance criteria from actively documenting conformance**

  - Impact Ratings - [**what does 0 mean**](https://github.com/w3c/sustainableweb-impact/pull/20)?

    - Alex - currently indeterminate. Many guidelines are difficult to measure, 0 is used in a fuzzy way. Feedback received and wording added on PR for review.

    - Discussion on PR, Tzviya to add suggestion to Github:

      - "Indeterminate ratings receive zero points to reflect uncertainty. Results rated as zero should not be excluded from assessment."
